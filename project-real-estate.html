Project Overview

This project involved designing and developing a website for a service specializing in surplus funds recovery (helping foreclosure and tax sale clients claim funds held by counties). The website’s core goal was to provide transparent information, establish trust in a sensitive legal recovery field, and convert claims inquiries through a secure submission form.

Developed using WordPress and Elementor, the platform features a clear typography scale, structured FAQ sections, and secure, responsive claim-evaluation forms.

Key Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Asset recovery services are often met with skepticism. The design needed to immediately look professional, secure, and clear, guiding users to understand their claim eligibility.

Solution: I built a comprehensive "How It Works" FAQ and process matrix and integrated trust-assurance elements such as privacy tags. The form includes specific fields allowing users to securely detail their tax sale or foreclosure background, which instantly routes to the agency's evaluation queue.

Core Features Implemented

  • Secure, trustworthy visual theme styled with professional teal/navy tones.
  • Interactive claim eligibility checklist.
  • Detailed FAQ toggle accordion for immediate trust building.
  • On-page SEO optimization for local search query reach.
  • Fully responsive and accessible layout forms.
